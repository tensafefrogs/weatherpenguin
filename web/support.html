The penguin says "Location Access Denied"

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Apps, then Weather Penguin, then Location, and choose "While Using the App".

The penguin is dressed wrong for me

Tap the gear in the top corner and move the sliders to choose when the penguin changes clothes. You can switch between °F and °C there too.

Can the penguin tell me what to wear in the morning?

Yes: tap the gear, turn on Morning penguin and pick a time. The notification is based on the latest forecast the app has, so open the app now and then to keep it up to date.

When does the penguin dress for rain?

Today: when it's raining now, there's at least a 25% chance of rain in the next hour, or at least a 35% chance today. Tomorrow: at least a 35% chance.
