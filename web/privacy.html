Weather Penguin

Privacy

Updated November 2026. Weather Penguin is made by EEG LLC ("we" on this page).

Weather Penguin doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. The app and this website count how they're used, but those analytics are anonymous and not tied to you (see Analytics).

Location

With your permission, the app uses your approximate location to get the forecast for where you are: iOS gives it an area a few kilometres across, never your exact position. That location is sent to Apple's weather service (Apple Weather / WeatherKit) to get the forecast (and, once, the usual temperatures there, so the penguin's starting temperatures suit your climate), and your phone looks up the name of your city. Your location is never sent to us, and the app doesn't keep a history of where you've been.

Settings

Your temperature settings and °F/°C choice are stored on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account so they follow you to your other devices. We can't see them.

Notifications

If you turn on Morning penguin, your phone schedules the notification itself from the latest forecast. Nothing is sent to us or to any notification service.

Analytics, not tied to you

The app and this website count how they're used with GoatCounter. These analytics are not tied to you: there's no account, user ID, advertising ID or cookie, and nothing links your visits over time or back to you. We only ever see totals. The one thing kept on your device is a note that the app has been opened before, so a first launch is counted only once; it never leaves your device.

What's counted:

  • That the app was opened, and whether it's the first time on that device
  • The language the app is in, and whether it shows °F or °C
  • Which outfit the penguin wore today (not the temperature or the forecast)
  • Whether you looked at Tomorrow, changed a setting, or turned Morning penguin on or off
  • The screen shape (iPhone, iPad or iPhone Duo) and any error the app showed
  • Which kinds of Weather Penguin widget you've added (not where or how often they're shown)
  • That the Apple Watch app was opened, that Siri or Shortcuts asked what to wear, or that a morning notification was tapped
  • On this website, which pages were visited

GoatCounter also notes the country, worked out from the IP address, which it doesn't store, and the type of device and browser. Your location, place name and weather are never sent. The analytics aren't used for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites, and they aren't sold or shared.
