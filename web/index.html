Weather Penguin

Weather Penguin is a cute, simple way to see what to wear today.

Free for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Try: rain cold warm hot

What to wear today

Open the app and a hand-drawn penguin is already dressed for the weather where you are: a sweater and scarf when it's cold, a jacket when it's cool, a T-shirt when it's warm and a bikini when it's hot. When rain is on the way, out come the raincoat, the poncho or the swim cap.

Features

  • Today and tomorrow Swipe to see what the penguin will wear tomorrow
  • Your temperatures Choose when the penguin switches from sweater to jacket to T-shirt
  • Morning penguin An optional notification each morning with what to wear
  • Widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen
  • Ask Siri what to wear
  • An Apple Watch app and watch face complications
  • °F or °C
  • iMessage stickers of every outfit
  • Your settings follow you to your other devices with iCloud
  • No account, no ads, no tracking

Weather data from Apple Weather.
